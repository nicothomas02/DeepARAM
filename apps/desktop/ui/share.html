Help improve DeepARAM?

You can share your finished ARAM Mayhem games to make the tier lists and builds better for everyone. It's off unless you turn it on.

What's shared

  • Champions, items, augments, runes and summoner spells
  • Kills, deaths, damage, gold, game length and who won

What's never shared

  • Summoner names, Riot IDs or account ids, yours or anyone else's
  • Chat, anything outside League, or which player you were

Each send is checked against your League sign-in so nobody can post fake games. The site keeps only a scrambled code for it, so turning sharing off removes the games you sent.

You can change this any time from DeepARAM's tray icon: Share match data.
