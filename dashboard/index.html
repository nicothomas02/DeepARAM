Share games anonymously

Off unless you turn it on. When on, each import also sends your games to help build stats across all players, such as matchups, augment combos and team comps. Shared games carry champions, items, augments, runes, spells, results and each player's gold and experience minute by minute, never names or account ids. Turning it off removes everything you shared.

Shared games build stats the site can't get any other way: runes, matchups, augment pairs and real game counts for every champion.

Save games to my account

Off unless you turn it on. When on, each import also saves your games to your deeparam.com account, so My Stats on your account page shows them on any device. Only you can see them. Turning it off removes them from your account.

Connect account

Connect the Riot account signed in to this computer to your deeparam.com account. Your account page opens in your browser: sign in there if you need to, then choose Connect. Only your Riot ID is connected; your games stay here unless you choose to save them.
